================================================================================
  1. WHAT IS THE BURSAR DASHBOARD?
================================================================================

The Bursar Dashboard is a dedicated workspace for the school bursar
(financial officer). It is separate from the School Admin dashboard —
the school admin has their own finance overview, but your dashboard
gives you deeper control over:

  - Fee structures and discounts
  - Invoice generation and tracking
  - Payment collection and recording
  - Expense tracking and budget monitoring
  - Staff payroll processing
  - Financial reporting and audit trails
  - Financial system settings

You have your own login, your own sidebar, and your own set of pages.
The school admin cannot see or change your settings, and you cannot
access school admin features like student enrollment or class management.

There is a "Switch to Admin" button in the sidebar if you also need
access to the school admin view.


================================================================================
  2. HOW YOU ACCESS IT
================================================================================

  URL:      /bursar
  Login:    Your own bursar credentials
  Role:     Bursar (shown as a gold badge in the sidebar)

Once logged in, you land on the Dashboard Overview page. All finance
pages are accessible from the left sidebar.

================================================================================
  12. DAY-TO-DAY WORKFLOWS (Step by Step)
================================================================================

Below are the main things you'd do on a typical day/week/month and
how the dashboard supports each one.

── FLOW 1: Morning Check-In — Monitor Financial Health ──

  1. Log in → land on Dashboard Overview
  2. Glance at the health badge (On Track / Needs Attention / Critical)
  3. Check the three KPI cards for collected, outstanding, and overdue
  4. Read any alert banners (upcoming deadlines, payroll reminders)
  5. Dismiss alerts you've already handled
  6. Review the revenue trend chart — toggle to 6M or 1Y for context
  7. Check the upcoming payments sidebar for anything due this week

── FLOW 2: Set Up or Adjust Fees for a New Term ──

  1. Go to Fee Management (sidebar) or Fee Structure tab
  2. Review existing fee categories and amounts per grade
  3. Click any category row to expand and see grade-by-grade pricing
  4. Click "Add Category" to create a new fee type
  5. Set the frequency (Per Term or Annual), mark as mandatory or optional
  6. Set amounts per grade level
  7. Review discounts — add new ones or adjust existing eligibility

── FLOW 3: Generate and Send Invoices ──

  1. Go to Invoices (sidebar) or Invoices tab
  2. Click "Generate Invoice" to create new invoices
  3. Select students, fee items, and applicable discounts
  4. Review the invoice preview (line items, subtotal, discounts, total)
  5. Auto-send is ON by default — invoices are emailed to parents
  6. Track invoice status in the table (Paid / Pending / Overdue / Draft)
  7. Click any invoice to open the detail panel and review
  8. Use "Send Reminder" for unpaid invoices

── FLOW 4: Record and Track Payments ──

  1. Go to Payments (sidebar)
  2. Click "Record Payment" to enter a new payment
  3. Use filters to find specific transactions (by status, type, method)
  4. Check the upcoming payments sidebar for due dates
  5. Select multiple transactions and send bulk reminders
  6. Download receipts for completed payments
  7. Review the collection rate gauge to see progress

── FLOW 5: Track Expenses and Monitor Budget ──

  1. Go to Expenses (sidebar) or Expenses tab
  2. Review budget summary cards (total, spent, committed, remaining)
  3. Check category-by-category budget bars — are any approaching limits?
  4. View the spending distribution donut chart for proportions
  5. Click "Add Expense" to record a new expense
  6. Filter the expense table by status or category
  7. Track pending expenses awaiting approval
  8. Budget alerts will trigger when spending reaches the threshold (80%)

── FLOW 6: Process Monthly Payroll ──

  1. Go to Payroll (sidebar) or Payroll tab
  2. Review summary: gross payroll, deductions, net payroll
  3. Check the department salary chart for distribution
  4. Review deductions breakdown (tax, pension, insurance)
  5. Search/filter staff if needed
  6. Click "Payslip" on any staff row to review their payslip
  7. Download or print individual payslips
  8. Click "Run Payroll" to process the current month
  9. System records the payment and updates the audit trail

── FLOW 7: Generate End-of-Term/Month Reports ──

  1. Go to Reports (sidebar) or Reports tab
  2. Select the report type you need:
     - Collection Summary — for fee collection overview
     - Revenue & Expenses — for income vs spending analysis
     - Outstanding Fees — for overdue accounts and aging
     - Audit Trail — for a complete activity log
  3. Review the report's KPI cards, charts, and tables
  4. For Audit Trail, filter by entity type or search specific entries
  5. Click "Export All" or "Download Log" to save as a file
  6. Share the report with school administration

── FLOW 8: Configure System Settings ──

  1. Go to Settings (sidebar)
  2. Adjust fiscal year, currency, or date format as needed
  3. Enable/disable payment methods or change gateway
  4. Configure late payment penalty rules
  5. Set invoice numbering and auto-send preferences
  6. Configure payment reminder schedule (which days, which channels)
  7. Set budget alert thresholds and approval rules
  8. Click "Save Changes"
